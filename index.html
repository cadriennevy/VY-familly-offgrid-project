Emergency playbook

  • Before a hazardCharge devices, refill water, secure loose items, and review the check-in plan.
  • During an evacuation orderFollow local authorities, bring go-bags, and use the family contact plan.
  • After a disruptionCheck injuries and hazards first; conserve supplies; log damage and updates.

Maintenance rhythm

Resilience ideas

  • Compact food gardenUse grow bags, vertical racks or containers for herbs and quick leafy crops.
  • Rainwater captureStart with clean gutters, screening and first-flush diversion; treat utility and drinking water differently.
  • Waste planSeparate rubbish, wet waste and sanitation supplies before collection is disrupted.

Build in layers

  • Layer 1Seven-day water, food, light, first aid, medication check and pet supplies.
  • Layer 2Power, communications, safe cooking, rain collection and repair supplies.
  • Layer 3Gardening, storage, preservation skills and regular practice.

Situation room

Use official sources first.

This app works offline, so it does not silently promise live alerts. Check official channels when a hazard is possible, then record a simple local update here.

Official hazard sources

  • PAGASA — weather advisories
  • NDRRMC — disaster updates
  • PHIVOLCS — earthquake & volcano information
  • Philippine Red Cross — emergency resources

When connectivity is limited
